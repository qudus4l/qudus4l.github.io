MY WORK

FIELD NOTE 01

Sharper-looking does not mean more usable.

A processed image can look more like a fingerprint without containing better evidence.

Early failures showed why the original photo matters. Enhancement can create convincing ridge-like patterns from blur, giving a matcher features that were never reliable evidence of the fingerprint.

The quality check examines the retained image without enhancement and checks the crop’s shape. Weak inputs are rejected before creating a template—the fingerprint features used for matching.
